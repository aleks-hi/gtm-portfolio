# Writing rules for outreach drafts

## Shape
- 3–4 sentences for a first touch, 2 for a last touch.
- One idea per sentence.
- One question, at the end, answerable in a line.

## Content
- Start with *their* situation: the trigger.
- Name a problem or goal they are likely to have *because of* the trigger.
- Talk about outcomes, not about your hours, rates or team size.
- Use only facts from the research log.

## Tone
- Like one professional writing to another: plain, warm, direct.
- No exclamation marks, no emojis.
- No flattery ("impressive growth", "love what you're doing").

## Banned phrases
- "just following up", "circling back", "touching base"
- "I hope this message finds you well"
- "quick question", "I'd love to pick your brain"
- "synergy", "leverage", "game-changer", "cutting-edge"
- "we are the leading…", "best-in-class"

## Language
- Write in the prospect's working language.
- Use the form of address that is normal in that language and industry.
