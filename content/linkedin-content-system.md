# LinkedIn content system for B2B

How I set up LinkedIn content so a stream of posts stays consistent, accurate and connected to outreach.

**Stack:** Claude (drafting, editing, translation, consistency checks) · LinkedIn · Canva · Google Sheets for content tracking

---

## 1. A repeatable post formula

1. **Hook:** a real problem the buyer recognises.
2. **What we do about it:** concrete and human, no buzzwords.
3. **Soft call to action:** an invitation to talk, never a hard sell.

**Rules:** 1–2 high-intent search phrases per post, worked in naturally. Short paragraphs, friendly and confident tone, no jargon. When a post format performs well, its structure becomes the template for the next posts.

## 2. Posts about people: facts only

When a post features an employee, include only what is verified (role, years of experience) and nothing attributed to them that they didn't say. The person should recognise every sentence.

## 3. Launch posts: one model, repeated everywhere

Build a product launch around one simple model, for example *what triggers the work → what the product does → who benefits*, and repeat it in every post and visual.

**Editing checklist:**
- Precise verbs; no vague "enables" or "gives a signal".
- One line that separates the product from "yet another tool".
- No two neighbouring sentences saying the same thing.
- **Text and visual reviewed together,** so the image shows the same model the copy describes.

## 4. Event posts for closed events

The post covers themes and format but has **no registration link.** Invitations go out personally, and direct messages handle the heads-ups and reminders.

## 5. Localising for a new market

Adapt rather than translate literally. A tracking sheet links original post → local-language text → design file, so a new market page starts with a full backlog.

## 6. From content to conversation

Posts build awareness, and direct messages start conversations. The message rules and research workflow are in [trigger-research-and-messaging.md](../outbound/trigger-research-and-messaging.md).
