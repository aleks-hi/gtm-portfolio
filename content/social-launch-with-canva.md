# Social media launch with Canva

How I set up social profiles for a small business that needs to be visible before it opens, plus the licence and compliance checks I run before anything is published.

**Stack:** Canva (design, Pro library, AI image generation) · Claude (layouts, copy, image processing, licence checks) · an AI image editor

---

## 1. Brand kit first

Derive everything from material the business already has, such as a printed flyer, so online and print match:

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Colours | Two: one main, one accent |
| Font | One headline font everywhere |
| Layouts | Three interchangeable post styles: white card, colour band, dark photo overlay |
| Logo | The real logo, never redrawn; check it isn't cut off by round profile frames |

Three fixed layouts make every new post a quick fill-in and keep the feed consistent.

## 2. Launch set

- Matching profiles on each platform: handle, bio, avatar, cover image.
- A first set of feed posts: the place, the offer, and recruitment if needed.
- A carousel and a short video with its own cover image.
- Captions that are specific, with clickable contact details and no filler.

## 3. When the location isn't finished yet

Edit photos with a strict prompt that removes only listed items (scaffolding, building materials) so the building stays true to life, say in the post that the image is edited, and swap in real photos once they exist.

## 4. Licence and compliance checklist

Checked against Canva's current licence terms, not from memory:

- **Free vs. Pro content:** free library photos generally come without model releases, so avoid recognisable faces in commercial posts.
- **Whose subscription:** a Pro licence on an employer's or third party's account doesn't cover your own client work. Use your own account or your own (or AI-generated) images.
- **No standalone media:** Pro images must be part of a design, so build text overlays in Canva, not on an exported bare photo.
- **Client handover:** add a short written clause limiting the client's use to their own purposes.
- **Local rules:** AI-content disclosure, written consent before showing real people, and any required business imprint on commercial profiles.
